#### **5. Our Use of Cookies and Tracking Technologies**

We utilize cookies and other tracking technologies like beacons and local storage to help our Services function and to understand user preferences.

*   **Cookies** are small text files stored by your browser that allow us to recognize you and store your preferences.
*   **Beacons** (or "pixels") are tiny electronic images that help us track activity on our Services.
*   **Local Storage** allows us to store data directly on your device to improve performance.

You can control and clear cookies through your browser's settings at any time.

Our use of these technologies falls into two categories:

| Category                     | Data Stored                               | Purpose                                                                     |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Essential Operations**     | Session identifiers, security tokens      | Manages user sessions, prevents security risks, and maintains site settings. |
| **Analytics & Functionality**| Anonymized user and device IDs, timestamps | Helps authenticate users, prevents server overload, and analyzes service usage. |
